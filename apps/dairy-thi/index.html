    The evidence

    Thresholds and context come from published work. The weather on this page is a sample.

    68

    THI at which milk yield of high-producing cows starts to fall; the older line was 72.

    Zimbelman et al. 2009 ↗
    10-12 kg

    Typical daily milk of Friesian Holstein cows in Indonesia, well below their potential; heat stress is a main cause.

    Review, Veterinary World 2023 ↗
    73-82

    THI measured at the national dairy breeding centre in Baturraden, enough to put cows at risk.

    BBPTU-SP Baturraden study ↗
    Altitude

    Lowland and lower-upland FH herds show moderate heat stress; upland herds much less.

    Buletin Peternakan, UGM ↗
