Basis

Salinity 20-35 ppt: no difference in Phronima growth across this range; slow drops are tolerated, sudden ones are the risk (Jannah et al., J. Fish Nutrition). Chlorella feed gives the best Phronima density (Herawati et al. 2020, Biodiversitas). Phronima suppa is endemic to brackish ponds in Suppa (Production of endemic Phronima suppa).
