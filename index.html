Selected work · 2026

Eight tools, live in the browser

Demos I built to show how I work. They run as plain static files, no server needed. Most use sample data, and each page says so.

Concept demo
01

Convective cell tracker

Storms that hit a town in the next hour start as cold cloud tops on satellite imagery.

Detects deep convective cells on real Himawari-9 infrared data, tracks them through merges and splits, projects 60 minutes ahead and warns towns in the path.

Method
Tb < 235 K, area ≥ 150 km², overlap tracking ≥ 25 %, 30-min motion
Built with
R · terra · sf · Parquet · Zarr

02

Cloud-top height in 3D

A flat infrared image hides how tall a storm really is.

Lifts every pixel to an estimated cloud-top height over real terrain and bathymetry, with the tracked cells drawn through time.

Method
Tropical lapse-rate profile, ETOPO1 relief
Built with
R · three.js · WebGL

Rain warning, South Sulawesi

A warning desk needs rain by district, the systems causing it and a message ready to send.

Rebuilt from my 2021 MEWS-SULSEL dashboard. Hourly rain for 307 sub-districts, satellite view, cluster tracking as a graph (GTG) and an auto-written public warning.

05

Rain gauge quality control

Raw gauge records are full of gaps, typos, stuck sensors and silent relocations.

Flags seven fault types, fills gaps from neighbours, finds breaks with SNHT and scores itself against the known truth. Clean CSV out.

Method
WMO-No. 100 and 1245, SNHT, normal-ratio filling
Built with
R · Monte Carlo · CSV

Flood atlas, Indonesia

Where floods hit, in which season, and how much La Niña and El Niño change the count.

Rebuilt from my 2018 flood map. Ten years of flood counts for 502 regencies, plus an inundation simulation on real Jakarta terrain and roads: tap a house to get its evacuation route and the best warning lead time.

Expertise

What I do

Most of my work falls into these four areas.

01

Satellite and radar

Himawari, GSMaP and radar: decoding, calibration, cloud detection and tracking.

02

Climate data quality

Station records checked, filled and homogenised to WMO guidance.

03

Early warning and risk

Heavy rain, drought, flood and marine hazard maps for the warning desk.

04

Agriculture and livestock

Crop phenology, pond water and heat stress in herds, read from weather data.

Specific tasks

  • Replicate input dataRebuild the dataset behind a study or report from its original sources.
  • Replicate a methodTurn a paper's method into working, tested code.
  • Replicate resultsReproduce published figures and tables, and explain any difference.
  • DigitisePaper records, scanned maps and charts into usable data.
  • VectoriseRaster to clean polygons: cloud cells, flood extent, fields.
  • Convert formatsBetween grids, tables, vectors and databases, metadata kept.
  • Training dataLabelled, tiled sets with splits that respect time and space.
  • Store and queryChunked arrays, columnar tables and databases that load fast.
  • Clean and checkGaps, typos, stuck sensors and relocations, scored against truth.
  • AutomateScheduled download, process and publish chains. oto_monitoring_himawari · chiba_himawari_8 · gsmap

About

Yosik Norman

Meteorologist in Indonesia since 2010. Physics degree in 2012, master's in earth sciences in 2017. I work mostly in R, with Bash, Fortran and GrADS for the heavy lifting.

Most days I am working with raw satellite, radar or station data, getting it into a shape a forecaster, a farmer or a planner can use.
